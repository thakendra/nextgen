CLASSICAL HOME DESIGNER IN NEPAL: ARCHITECTURE, INTERIORS, CONSTRUCTION & CONSULTING

August 16, 2026 11 min read By NextGen Team

Classical and neoclassical home design represents timeless prestige, grand proportions, and meticulous craftsmanship. As Nepal's leading classical home designer, NextGen Interiors & Architects offers end-to-end services — covering neoclassical architectural drafting, luxury classical interior fit-outs, turnkey construction, and DPR financial consulting across Nepal.

01 COMPLETE ALL-IN-ONE CLASSICAL HOME SOLUTIONS

Building a classical residence in Kathmandu, Lalitpur, Bhaktapur, or Pokhara requires specialized expertise in traditional geometry and European/Neoclassical detailing:

  • Neoclassical Exterior Architectural Design — Symmetrical facades, grand entry archways, Ionic/Corinthian columns, and ornate balustrades.
  • Luxury Classical Interior Fit-Outs — Coffered ceilings, wainscoting paneling, brass fittings, custom veneer joinery, and Italian marble flooring.
  • Turnkey Building Construction — Heavy RCC structural framing, specialized plaster moldings, and weather-resistant facade detailing.
  • DPR & Architectural Consulting — Fixed itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ), municipal code approval drawings, and site supervision (see our DPR & Landscaping Services).

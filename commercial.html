COMMERCIAL INTERIOR & ARCHITECTURE FAQS

What commercial projects does NextGen specialize in?

We design retail stores, corporate showrooms, commercial complexes, banks, wellness clinics, and entertainment venues engineered for high foot traffic, brand visibility, and operational efficiency.

Do you handle commercial brand identity integration?

Yes, our commercial design team integrates your company's branding, color psychology, custom retail display fixtures, and facade signage seamlessly into the spatial design.

How do you ensure safety and building compliance?

All commercial blueprints adhere strictly to Nepal National Building Code (NBC), including emergency egress routes, fire-rated partitions, HVAC zoning, and accessible design provisions.

What is the timeline for commercial interior fit-outs?

Commercial fit-outs generally range from 30 to 90 days depending on scale, floor area, and custom fabrication specifications, with scheduled day and night shifts to meet commercial launch dates.

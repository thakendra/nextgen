INTERIOR ARCHITECTURE & DESIGN FAQS

What is the difference between hiring a decorator and NextGen Interior Architects?

NextGen provides comprehensive spatial engineering—we reconfigure layouts, design bespoke electrical and lighting circuits, engineer HVAC and plumbing drops, and design custom joinery rather than just choosing loose surface decor.

Do you provide 3D photorealistic renderings before construction starts?

Yes, every project includes detailed 3D visualizations, material swatches, and lighting simulations so you can experience your space with precision before a single brick or panel is built.

How do you ensure interior finishes match the approved 3D renders?

We operate our own precision woodworking and fabrication facilities with skilled craftsmen, strict material grading (marine ply, acrylic/PU laminates, imported hardware), and dedicated site supervisors.

What materials and hardware brands do you use in Nepal?

We specify genuine marine-grade plywood (Gurjan core), hardware from Häfele, Hettich, and Blum, premium laminates from Merino and Advance, and sanitary fixtures from Kohler and Roca.

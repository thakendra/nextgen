ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN & ENGINEERING FAQS

What is NextGen's full architectural service package in Nepal?

Our architectural scope includes site analysis, conceptual 3D massing, detailed floor plans, structural engineering drawings, MEP blueprints, municipal approval documentation, and on-site construction supervision.

Are your structural designs earthquake resilient?

Every building design complies with Nepal National Building Code (NBC 105:2020) for seismic resistance, engineered by certified structural engineers using advanced analysis tools.

What architectural styles do you specialize in?

We craft contemporary minimalist, modern brutalist, neo-classical European, and Nepalese vernacular fusions tailored to each client's aesthetic preferences and climatic context.

How do you obtain municipal building permits in Kathmandu Valley?

Our liaison team prepares compliant municipal submission drawings, structural certification reports, and coordinates directly with local ward and municipal offices for permit sign-offs.

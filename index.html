FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

NextGen is a premier multidisciplinary architecture, structural engineering, interior design, and turnkey construction company based in Baluwatar, Kathmandu. We deliver high-end residential homes, luxury villas, boutique hotels, commercial offices, banquets, and healthcare environments from preliminary concept and 3D visualization through municipal permit approvals to finished turnkey construction.

Our design studio and corporate headquarters are located on Thirbam Sadak, Baluwatar, Kathmandu. While based in Kathmandu, NextGen operates nationwide across Nepal, with completed and ongoing landmark architectural and interior projects in Lalitpur, Bhaktapur, Pokhara, Chitwan (Bharatpur), Dang, Surkhet, Biratnagar, and Butwal.

A turnkey contract provides single-point accountability from architectural conception to keys handover. It prevents budget escalation, resolves design discrepancies between architects and contractors before construction begins, ensures strict quality control on imported materials and bespoke joinery, and guarantees timely project delivery within agreed milestones.

Every architectural and structural drawing produced by NextGen is fully certified by licensed Nepal Engineering Council (NEC) engineers to comply with NBC 105:2020 seismic standards, municipal bylaws, FAR regulations, and safety mandates for municipal building permits across all municipalities in Nepal.

The timeline depends on the scale and scope of the project. For residential architecture, structural engineering, and municipal permit drawings, the design phase typically takes 4 to 8 weeks. For interior design and turnkey fit-outs, concept design and 3D visualization take 2 to 4 weeks, followed by 6 to 12 weeks for on-site execution, bespoke carpentry, and turnkey handover.

We partner directly with leading certified brands including German hardware systems (Häfele, Hettich), premium marine-grade laminates, commercial vitrified tiles, sanitaryware from Kohler and Roca, and seasoned grade-A hardwood. Every component is crafted in our centralized workshop and verified on-site to ensure structural durability, fire safety, and timeless luxury.

You can start by booking an initial design consultation with our principal architects. We review your land survey or floor plan, discuss your aesthetic vision, functional requirements, and target budget, and formulate a customized architectural roadmap and fee estimate within 2 to 3 business days.

HOTEL & HOSPITALITY DESIGN FAQS

What types of hospitality developments do you handle in Nepal?

We provide architecture and interior design for luxury boutique hotels, mountain resorts, banquet halls, restaurants, cafés, and wellness retreats across Kathmandu, Pokhara, Surkhet, and regional tourism corridors.

How do you balance guest experience with back-of-house operations?

We engineer strict operational separation between guest circulation and service zones, optimizing commercial kitchen layouts, housekeeping logistics, laundry chutes, and storage paths.

Can NextGen assist with resort master planning and terrain analysis?

Yes, we perform comprehensive topographical analysis, passive climate planning, view-corridor orientation, and eco-villa zoning for large resort master plans in hilly and riverside terrains.

How does interior design impact hotel revenue and guest reviews?

Thoughtful space planning, ergonomic room layouts, acoustic privacy, and signature photo-worthy design moments directly drive higher average daily rates (ADR), repeat bookings, and five-star social reviews.

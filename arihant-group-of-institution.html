Project Overview

MODERN OFFICE

Arihant Group of Institution Office is a commercial workspace project designed by NextGen Interiors in Kalanki, Kathmandu. The spatial layout optimizes professional workflows with dedicated zones, high-functionality workstations, and strategic ambient lighting. Featuring a refined material palette and sleek contemporary finishes, this project sets a high standard for modern office interior design and workspace planning in Kathmandu, Nepal.

Designed and executed with bespoke craftsmanship in KALANKI , KATHMANDU. Our team prioritized spatial flow, natural ventilation, purposeful illumination, and durable, premium materials to create an enduring environment tailored for modern lifestyle and architectural excellence.

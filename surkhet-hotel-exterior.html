Project Overview

Where Nepali Heritage Meets Timeless Hospitality

A beautiful fusion of Nepal’s rich architectural heritage, traditional details, and contemporary design. Architecturally engineered to showcase timeless regional aesthetic standards, Surkhet Hotel Exterior is a commercial hospitality architecture project designed by NextGen Interiors & Architects in Surkhet, Karnali, Nepal. The exterior facade balances traditional Nepali architectural heritage and intricate wooden details with clean, modern building lines to create a striking visual identity.

Focusing on commercial facade design, high-rise hospitality architecture, and durable exterior materials, the project optimizes elevation layout and outdoor lighting. The design sets a refined benchmark for hotel architecture, commercial building facade design, and architectural exterior design in Nepal.

Designed and executed with bespoke craftsmanship in SURKHET, KARNALI,NEPAL. Our team prioritized spatial flow, natural ventilation, purposeful illumination, and durable, premium materials to create an enduring environment tailored for modern lifestyle and architectural excellence.

HEALTHCARE & CLINIC DESIGN FAQS

What standards do you follow for medical and clinic interiors?

We adhere to infection-control protocols, non-porous antimicrobial surface materials, sterile zoning, HEPA-filtered HVAC layouts, and seamless medical gas and power trunking.

How do you design patient-friendly medical waiting areas?

We employ calming biophilic palettes, acoustic dampening, indirect glare-free illumination, and intuitive wayfinding to alleviate patient anxiety and provide welcoming family spaces.

Do you handle specialized imaging or dental clinic equipment requirements?

Yes, our team plans lead-lined radiation shielding for X-ray/CT rooms, reinforced slab loads for dental chairs, and specialized medical vacuum and plumbing infrastructure.

How do you manage barrier-free accessibility in clinics?

We integrate zero-threshold entryways, automated sliding doors, wide hospital-grade corridors, and ADA-compliant accessible restrooms throughout the facility.

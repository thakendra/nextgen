INTERIOR ARCHITECTURE & CONSTRUCTION

INTERIOR ARCHITECTURE DESIGN & TURNKEY CONSTRUCTION IN NEPAL

August 16, 2026 12 min read By NextGen Team

Building modern commercial, corporate, or luxury residential spaces in Nepal requires a unified approach. At NextGen Interiors & Architects, we specialize in interior architecture design & construction in Nepal — seamlessly managing building design, interior space planning, DPR cost control, and on-site construction supervision under one roof.

01 THE ADVANTAGE OF TURNKEY INTERIOR ARCHITECTURE & CONSTRUCTION

In traditional construction, property owners hire separate architects, interior designers, MEP engineers, and general contractors. This fragmented structure frequently causes miscommunication, delayed schedules, and costly budget overruns. Turnkey interior architecture eliminates these friction points:

  • Single Point of Accountability — NextGen takes total responsibility for design precision, material quality, and execution deadlines.
  • Seamless Design-to-Build Transition — 3D interior concepts and architectural blueprints are directly implemented by our in-house master craftsmen and site engineers.
  • Precise DPR & Cost Certainty — Comprehensive itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with fixed pricing guarantees zero unexpected expenses.
  • Advanced Structural & MEP Integration — Electrical, HVAC ducting, plumbing, and structural modifications planned before fit-out begins.

02 SECTORS WE SERVE ACROSS NEPAL

NextGen provides turnkey interior architecture and construction across every major sector:

  • Corporate & Workplace Fit-Outs — Executive cabins, open workstation layouts, reception lounges, and conference rooms (e.g. Vanimandal Office).
  • Commercial & Retail Stores — Luxury showrooms, salons, and retail destinations (e.g. Skin Care Center & Babarmahal Pharmacy).
  • Luxury Residential & Villa Construction — Custom homes, private villas, and duplex renovations across Kathmandu, Bhaktapur, Chitwan, & Lalitpur.
  • Hospitality & Event Venues — Hotels, banquet halls, resorts, and restaurants (e.g. Surkhet Banquet Hall).
Combining architectural structure with interior craftsmanship ensures every space is structurally sound, visually extraordinary, and commercially productive.

03 OUR INTEGRATED 4-STAGE EXECUTION WORKFLOW

1. Architectural Survey & Space Planning — Analyzing structural parameters, load-bearing requirements, and spatial flow.

2. Photorealistic 3D Interior & Exterior Rendering — Complete virtual walkthroughs with precise lighting and material finishes.

3. DPR & Engineering Documentation — Comprehensive structural blueprints, BOQs, and municipal compliance drawings.

4. On-Site Turnkey Construction & Supervision — Professional site engineering, material installation, and final quality control handover.

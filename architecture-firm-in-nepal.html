THE NEXTGEN 8-STEP ARCHITECTURAL WORKFLOW

We eliminate ambiguity from building construction. Our structured 8-step methodology guarantees that architectural vision translates flawlessly into solid structural reality on budget and on schedule.

01

Site Survey & Brief

Site visits, boundary verification, road width analysis, soil reports, and in-depth client design brief formulation.

02

Concept 3D Massing

Developing spatial zoning, volumetric studies, sun-path alignments, and primary architectural elevations.

03

Seismic Engineering

Full structural FEM modeling adhering strictly to Nepal National Building Code NBC 105:2020 for earthquake safety.

04

MEP & Working Drawings

Comprehensive blueprints: electrical line diagrams, plumbing risers, HVAC ducting, and structural rebar details.

05

Photorealistic 3D VR

Hyper-realistic exterior renders, daytime/nighttime architectural lighting simulations, and 3D virtual walkthroughs.

06

Municipal DPC Approvals

Preparation of municipality submission blueprints (Naksha Pass), structural safety certifications, and ward liaison.

07

BOQ & Cost Estimation

Transparent itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ), material specifications, and tender documents to prevent contractor overruns.

08

On-Site Supervision

Regular site engineer inspections at rebar checking, slab casting, brickwork, and turnkey handover verification.

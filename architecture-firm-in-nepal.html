Our Architecture Design Process

1. Initial Consultation

We begin by understanding your project: project type, site location, land size, requirements, preferred style, approximate scope, budget considerations and development objectives.

2. Site Analysis

The site influences almost every architectural decision — orientation, access, surrounding buildings, views, topography, sunlight, ventilation, climate, existing conditions and local planning requirements.

3. Concept Development

We develop the architectural concept from the consultation and site analysis, establishing the design language, massing and spatial direction of the project.

4. Architectural Planning & Design

The approved concept becomes a detailed architectural proposal: floor planning, elevations, sections, facade development and other architectural drawings.

5. 3D Visualisation

3D visualisation helps clients understand the proposed building before construction — exterior appearance, massing, materials, facade, landscape relationship, lighting and spatial character.

6. Documentation & DPR

Detailed documentation and DPR requirements are developed according to the scope of the project.

7. Approvals & Coordination

Where applicable, the design process incorporates coordination related to municipal approvals and project requirements.

8. Construction Coordination

The architectural design is coordinated with execution so the final result stays aligned with the intended design.

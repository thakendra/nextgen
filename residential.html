RESIDENTIAL ARCHITECTURE & HOME DESIGN FAQS

What does NextGen's residential design service in Nepal include?

Our residential services encompass architectural planning, 3D interior visualization, structural & MEP engineering, municipal drawing approvals, custom millwork, curated lighting, and turnkey construction management for private homes, villas, and apartments across Nepal.

How do you handle house design and construction outside Kathmandu?

NextGen operates nationwide. We have active and completed residential architecture and interior projects in Pokhara, Chitwan, Bhaktapur, Lalitpur, Dang, and Surkhet, with our core site supervision and project coordination teams ensuring consistent quality standards.

What is the typical timeline for designing and building a luxury home?

Design and municipal drawings typically take 4 to 8 weeks depending on custom requirements. Construction and turnkey interior fit-outs typically span 6 to 14 months, managed with structured milestone reporting.

Can NextGen assist with interior renovations for existing houses?

Yes, we provide full structural and interior retrofitting, space re-planning, electrical and plumbing modernization, and turnkey finish upgrades for existing residential properties.

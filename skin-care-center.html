Project Overview

A Perfect Blend of Beauty, Comfort & Modern Elegance.

Designed for beauty and wellness, Skin Care Center in KL Tower, Bouddha, is a premium commercial interior project by NextGen Interiors and architects . The space features soft ambient lighting, a calming color palette, and ergonomic treatment layouts to maximize client comfort and operational flow. Every detail creates a serene, upscale environment, setting a high standard for aesthetic clinic and skincare center interior design in Kathmandu, Nepal.

Designed and executed with bespoke craftsmanship in KL TOWER , BOUDDHA, KATHMANDU, NEPLA. Our team prioritized spatial flow, natural ventilation, purposeful illumination, and durable, premium materials to create an enduring environment tailored for modern lifestyle and architectural excellence.

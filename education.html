EDUCATIONAL FACILITY DESIGN FAQS

What educational facilities does NextGen design?

We design schools, colleges, specialized training academies, STEM laboratories, libraries, auditoriums, and child-safe kindergarten environments across Nepal.

How do you incorporate safety and accessibility in school architecture?

Our designs feature wide emergency staircases, anti-slip flooring, rounded-edge furnishings, universal wheelchair ramps, and secure perimeter access control.

How do modern classroom layouts support active learning?

We design flexible, reconfigurable modular furniture, interactive digital display walls, optimal natural cross-ventilation, and acoustic ceiling baffles to facilitate collaborative group learning.

Do you design outdoor learning and sports amenities?

Yes, our educational master planning integrates multipurpose playgrounds, covered assembly pavilions, amphitheaters, and green courtyards.
